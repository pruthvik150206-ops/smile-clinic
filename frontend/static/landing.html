Frequently Asked Questions

What should I bring for my first consultation? +
Please bring a valid photo ID, details of any existing medical conditions or allergies, and any past dental/ENT X-rays or diagnostic reports if available.
Are root canals and teeth whitening painful? +
At SmileClinic, we use laser whitening technology with built-in desensitizing agents and rotary endodontics for root canals to ensure 100% comfort.
How do I access my patient records and prescriptions? +
All diagnostic notes, X-rays, prescriptions, and billing invoices are automatically uploaded to your secure Patient Portal at smile-clinic-theta.vercel.app.
