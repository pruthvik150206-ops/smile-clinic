Our approach

We believe that exceptional care is patient-centred, unhurried, and built on trust — not just clinical outcomes.

SmileClinic brings together dental and ENT specialists under one roof with a shared philosophy: treat the whole person, explain every step, and leave you genuinely better. From a routine cleaning to complex surgical planning, the standard of care and attention never changes.

I
Specialist led
Every consultation is led by a qualified specialist. No junior stand-ins, no diagnostic guesswork.
II
Evidence based
Protocols grounded in current clinical evidence and continuously updated as the field evolves.
III
Unhurried care
Appointment times that allow a proper consultation, not a rushed examination.

Specialties

Comprehensive care across dental and ENT disciplines.

Dental
Preventive Dentistry
Scaling, polishing, fluoride application, and comprehensive oral exams to protect your teeth before problems arise.
Dental
Restorative Care
Tooth-coloured fillings, ceramic crowns, bridges and root canal therapy that restore function and aesthetics.
Dental
Cosmetic Dentistry
Laser whitening, porcelain veneers, and smile design procedures for results that feel natural, not theatrical.
Dental
Orthodontics
Fixed braces and clear aligner treatment for children and adults, planned and monitored by a specialist orthodontist.
ENT
Ear & Hearing
Diagnosis and management of ear infections, hearing loss, tinnitus, wax accumulation, and balance disorders.
ENT
Nasal & Sinus
Allergic rhinitis, sinusitis, deviated nasal septum, nasal polyps, and functional endoscopic sinus surgery.
ENT
Throat & Voice
Assessment and treatment of tonsillitis, voice disorders, swallowing difficulties, and laryngeal conditions.
ENT
ENT Surgery
Tonsillectomy, adenoidectomy, septoplasty and functional sinus procedures performed with precision in an equipped theatre.
Dental
Oral Surgery
Wisdom tooth removal, minor oral surgical procedures, and post-extraction care under local or conscious sedation.
Your first visit

What to expect when you come to us.

We have designed every part of the visit to feel calm, clear, and free of unnecessary waiting. Here is what a typical appointment looks like.

01
Arrival & registration
Check in at reception. Your pre-filled information means you spend less time on paperwork and more with your specialist.
02
Specialist consultation
A thorough examination with time set aside for your questions. We explain every finding in plain language.
03
Diagnosis & plan
A clear treatment plan, including options and their costs, before any procedure is started. Your consent, always.
04
Ongoing care
Consultation notes and follow-up recommendations are accessible through your patient portal after every visit.
